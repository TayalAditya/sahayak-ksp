# Karnataka State Police — Legal Reference for Crime Intelligence

## IPC to BNS Transition (effective 1 July 2024)

The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) replaced the Indian Penal Code (IPC) on 1 July 2024.
FIRs registered before 1 July 2024 use IPC sections. FIRs registered on or after use BNS sections.

## Special Acts (unchanged)

- NDPS Act Section 20: Drug offences (possession/transportation of narcotics)
- IT Act Section 66D: Cyber fraud / cheating by personation
- POCSO Act Section 8: Child sexual assault

## Investigation Procedure (BNSS)

- BNSS replaced CrPC on 1 July 2024
- FIR = First Information Report, filed at the police station of jurisdiction
- Charge Sheet (Final Report) must be filed within 60 days of arrest (magistrate trial) or 90 days (Sessions Court)
- Bail: Sections 480-483 BNSS govern bail; cognizable offences require magistrate sanction

## Repeat Offender Definition

A person with 3 or more accused roles across distinct FIRs is classified as a repeat/habitual offender.
Risk scoring considers: number of cases, recency, crime severity, bail status.
